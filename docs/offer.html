Hospital Price Series · Brief · Sources

Monthly MRF-change extract

A dated cash-plus-named-payer CSV for CPT codes you name, from a public hospital machine-readable file. When that hospital’s published file changes (last_updated_on or the zip Last-Modified), you get the new extract.

What you get

  • One CSV per cycle: hospital name, last-updated date, extract date, description, CPT, setting, billing class, gross, discounted cash, and named-payer negotiated-dollar columns that appear in the file.
  • The CPT list you send (up to the codes that actually exist in that MRF). We do not invent codes.
  • A short note of which source index (cms-hpt.txt) and which MRF URL were used.
  • Delivery through Ko-fi messages. Built with the public hospital-mrf-index Action and shoppable-extract.

A public sample from Flagstaff Medical Center (full file, 8 CPTs, not a quote) is in the repo: data/fmc_shoppable_sample_2026-08-25.csv.

What this is not

  • Not a patient quote, bill, allowed amount, or coverage determination.
  • Not advice to buy insurance, switch plans, or negotiate a claim.
  • Not an endorsement by CMS or any hospital. The hospital owns the listed files.
